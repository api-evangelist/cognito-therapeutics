# Cognito Therapeutics

> Clinical-stage neurotechnology company in Cambridge, Massachusetts, founded in 2016 out of MIT research by Ed Boyden and Li-Huei Tsai. Its investigational product Spectris is a non-invasive at-home neuromodulation device delivering synchronized 40Hz light and sound stimulation to evoke gamma neural activity and slow decline in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease. Spectris AD holds FDA Breakthrough Device Designation.
